Active Cyber Operations

The DSI engages in both defensive and proactive cyber operations to secure the nation's critical infrastructure. Operational details are highly classified, but unclassified summaries of our defensive posture are provided below.

Defensive Posture (Defend Forward)

We actively hunt for malicious activity within domestic networks before it reaches critical systems. Our zero-trust implementation ensures that no node is inherently trusted.
