Intelligence Divisions

The DSI operates multiple specialized divisions dedicated to specific threat vectors and intelligence gathering disciplines. These divisions work in tandem to produce the President's Daily Cyber Brief (PDCB).

Signals Intelligence (SIGINT)

Monitoring, intercepting, and deciphering communications and electronic signals from foreign adversaries. The SIGINT division employs advanced cryptanalysis and operates some of the most sophisticated supercomputing clusters in the federal government.
